Budlark is an early, unsigned Windows app for trying tiny, routine-linked habits, check-ins and a personal growing garden. This version is for designated owner testing only; it has not been verified for general use. You can plant in a local device garden without signing in; account gardens and sync require sign-in. Some features and sign-in options are still being refined. It is not a complete release or medical care. There is no Microsoft Store version yet. Private feedback is available in the app after sign-in.

What you can try

Budlark helps you connect one small action to a routine you already have, check in when you try it, and reflect on what fits. Your garden grows as you practice; missing a day does not take that growth away.

You can plant in a local device garden without signing in. Sign-in is required for an account garden and cloud sync; some features and sign-in options are still being refined. The app is designed for ages 16 and up, is not medical care, and is not a complete release. There is no Microsoft Store version yet.

Cloud sync may be interrupted or temporarily unavailable. Your local device garden stays on that device; signing in and syncing an account garden require an internet connection.

Your records can be exported and deleted in Settings. Some deletion requests wait until the device can sync. The app's local database is not encrypted. Read the privacy, retention and recovery details before using the preview.

For support or privacy requests, email store-developer@outlook.com; you do not need to sign in. Do not include passwords or sensitive habit details. For non-sensitive bug reports only, use Budlark public issues. Reports there are public and require a GitHub account.

Recent preview changes

One Windows installer

The current preview has one installer for 64-bit Windows PCs. It chooses the appropriate version for x64 or ARM64 devices. Installation can happen offline, but signing in requires an internet connection.

More control over your records

Settings includes export and deletion controls for your garden and private feedback. Deletion may wait until a device reconnects, and service backups may retain copies for an unknown time.

Optional choices stay optional

Website counts are off until you opt in. Optional in-app measurements are also off by default. Page comparisons are off on the public website.
