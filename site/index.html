Your habits are private.

What stays on your device

SQLite stores recipes, check-in edits, reflection scores, feedback and settings. Authentication credentials use Windows secure storage, never the database. Your Windows account's filesystem protections apply; SQLite itself is not encrypted.

What sync sends

When signed in and connected, recipes, check-ins, reflections, feedback and safe account preferences sync to your private account partition. Analytics consent, reminder enablement and startup stay device-local. Feedback and ratings are private, not published to an ideas board. The identity broker stores the basic identity attributes needed for Microsoft, Google or email sign-in.

What we never do

No ads, no AI, no selling habit data. Optional event counts are off by default and contain no habit or feedback text. Invitation links contain no personal garden data.

Export your records as JSON from Settings. Delete local data or request cloud data deletion from the connected app. Cloud deletion keeps a minimal account deletion marker to prevent old devices from restoring your deleted data. Identity provider accounts are managed by their providers, not by Bloomstep.

Raw opt-in app event retention is 13 months. Optional app diagnostics record at most 10 observed Dart/Flutter errors per session: fixed category/source only, never messages, stack traces or habit/account text. They may be nonfatal; native/process deaths are not captured, so no crash-free rate is inferred. No product telemetry is collected by this website. Microsoft Azure hosts this website, the identity broker and the private account database; GitHub hosts source, build artifacts and downloads. Those providers can retain standard access/security logs. There are no third-party product analytics or AI processors. A full controller/contact and data-processing notice remains a public-MVP launch gate.

Cloud engineering-preview storage and request quotas are finite. A quota or service pause is shown explicitly; your local garden remains available. These volume controls are not a guarantee that Azure free allowances can never be exceeded. No paid tier is selected.

Use the private in-app feedback channel for sensitive support once it is connected. Do not place private habits, credentials or account exports in public GitHub issues. Public source and non-sensitive bug reports: Bloomstep repository.
