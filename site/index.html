Your habits are private.

What stays on your device

SQLite stores recipes, check-in edits, reflection scores, feedback and settings. Authentication credentials use Windows secure storage, never the database. Your Windows account's filesystem protections apply; SQLite itself is not encrypted.

What sync sends

When signed in and connected, recipes, check-ins, reflections, feedback and safe account preferences sync to your private account partition. Analytics consent, reminder enablement and startup stay device-local. Feedback and ratings are private, not published to an ideas board. The service records server receipt and first committed team-reply times alongside new private feedback. Historical missing times are not backfilled. Service-operational support records are separate from optional product-event analytics and are removed with their feedback records. The identity broker stores the basic identity attributes needed for Microsoft, Google or email sign-in.

What we never do

No ads, no AI, no selling habit data. Optional event counts are off by default and contain no habit or feedback text. Invitation links contain no personal garden data.

Export your records as JSON from Settings. Preview.5 adds confirmed owner-only deletion for an individual recipe and its check-ins/reflections/linked events, or private feedback/rating and its replies. Offline requests queue until sync; other devices apply deletion when they reconnect. Permanent minimal ID-only record markers prevent stale copies from returning and appear in exports; preview limits do not silently prune them. Whole-account cloud deletion separately keeps its minimal account marker. Earlier exported copies and processor backup retention are separate, not an instantaneous purge promise. Identity provider accounts are managed by their providers, not by Bloomstep.

Raw opt-in app event retention is 13 months. Optional app diagnostics record at most 10 observed Dart/Flutter errors per session: fixed category/source only, never messages, stack traces or habit/account text. They may be nonfatal; native/process deaths are not captured, so no crash-free rate is inferred. Optional website observations stay in this browser for up to seven days unless you export and explicitly link them in the app; this site has no analytics upload endpoint. Clear browser site storage to remove local receipts; exported copies must be deleted separately. Microsoft Azure hosts this website, the identity broker and the private account database; GitHub hosts source, build artifacts and downloads. Those providers can retain standard access/security logs. There are no third-party product analytics or AI processors. A full controller/contact and data-processing notice remains a public-MVP launch gate.

Cloud engineering-preview storage and request quotas are finite. A quota or service pause is shown explicitly; your local garden remains available. These volume controls are not a guarantee that Azure free allowances can never be exceeded. No paid tier is selected.

Bloomstep uses Microsoft Entra External ID for secure browser sign-in. The Microsoft service domain is ciamlogin.com; Google may show “continue to ciamlogin.com” while provider branding is unverified. That label is not a request to share your password with Bloomstep. Complete credentials only on your chosen provider's genuine page; Bloomstep never collects your password.

Use the private in-app feedback channel for sensitive support once it is connected. Do not place private habits, credentials or account exports in public GitHub issues. Public source and non-sensitive bug reports: Bloomstep repository.
