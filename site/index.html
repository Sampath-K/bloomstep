For the curious · The thinking behind tiny

Don’t ask for more willpower.
Make the next step smaller.

BJ Fogg’s Behavior Model says a behavior happens when motivation, ability and a prompt come together at the same moment. Making an action easier addresses ability; an existing routine can be your prompt. Explore Fogg’s original model →

BBehavior=MMotivationAAbilityPPrompt

Design the moment, not a new personality.

For our example, the mug is the prompt, one sentence makes the action easier, and writing something you care about supplies a reason to begin. This is an illustration of B=MAP, not a prediction or a numerical equation.

Why tiny? Make repetition easier.

Fogg’s model identifies simplicity as a way to make behavior easier to do. A separate real-world habit-formation study by Lally and colleagues found that repeating a behavior in a consistent context was associated with increasing automaticity. Fogg’s ability model · Lally et al., 2010, European Journal of Social Psychology.

In that 12-week study, modeled time to reach 95% of the automaticity plateau varied from 18 to 254 days among participants whose data fit the model; longer times were modeled, not observed throughout. There is no fixed “21-day” promise here, and the study did not test Bloomstep or establish that tiny actions or celebration caused those outcomes. Read the research.

A few things you might be wondering.

What if I miss a day?

Your plant does not wilt and your progress is not taken away. Come back when you can, or make the action smaller.

Is Bloomstep free?

This owner-test preview is free; there is no subscription or payment step. It is not verified for general use.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

You need to sign in to get started. Once your garden is available on the device, you can work locally during a connection interruption and sync when connected again. Signing in and syncing need an internet connection, and online service may be interrupted. Sign-in options depend on the available providers.

Why might sign-in show another service name?

Bloomstep uses a sign-in service from Microsoft. Depending on your chosen provider, the sign-in page may show a Microsoft service address such as ciamlogin.com. Enter your password only on the genuine Google or Microsoft sign-in page you chose; Bloomstep does not collect your password.

How can I share feedback or ask for help?

Email store-developer@outlook.com for support or privacy requests, including when you cannot sign in. After sign-in, you can also use the app's private feedback option. Do not include passwords or sensitive habit details. For a non-sensitive bug, use the project's public GitHub issues; posting requires a GitHub account and the report is public. Never post private records, account exports, credentials or privacy requests there. Feedback is not an email signup, and a reply or response time is not guaranteed. This website does not collect contact details or offer a mailing list.

Can I take my habits with me or delete them?

Use Settings to export your records as JSON. The app also has deletion controls for habits, private feedback and your cloud account data. Offline deletion requests wait for sync; exported files and provider backups are separate copies.

Is this medical advice?

No. Bloomstep is a habit-building tool, not medical care or treatment. Choose actions that are safe for you. It is designed for ages 16 and up.

What is processed and why

When you sign in, Bloomstep syncs your recipes, check-ins, reflections, feedback and basic account settings to your private account. Your sign-in provider handles your sign-in and the identity details it requests; Bloomstep uses the returned identity to give you account access. Bloomstep also keeps service records about when feedback arrives and when a team reply is sent. These are separate from optional app measurements. Do not store medical records, passwords or other sensitive information in Bloomstep.

Website and app measurements are optional and are not required to download or use the app. If you opt in, they help us understand broad product activity; they are not advertising profiles. The website choice resets when you reload the page. The app choice is stored on your device. You can turn either choice off to stop future optional measurements. This does not stop information needed to provide your account or respond to a support request. This notice does not claim compliance with a particular privacy law or certification; the rights that apply depend on where you live and the law that applies.

Optional app measurements are off by default. If enabled, limited technical error reports may include an error category and where it occurred, but not the error message, technical trace or your habit or account text. Some unexpected app closures may not be captured, so these reports do not show how often the app crashes. A separate optional reminder choice may record when you enable or disable in-app reminders and whether they remain enabled after 30 days. It does not tell us whether Windows allowed or delivered a reminder, and it does not include your habit text, location or complete reminder history. Turning off either choice stops future records; previously synced records follow the retention and deletion rules below.

Retention and deletion boundaries

Optional app measurements are kept for 396 days from when the activity originally happened (13 months). Daily website counts and page-comparison records are kept for up to 90 days. Records of requests to Bloomstep's services and daily summaries are kept for 30 days. A temporary random code helps avoid duplicate website counts for up to five minutes, then is removed during subsequent service activity.

Your garden, feedback and settings do not expire because you stop using the app. They remain until you use the matching deletion control. Deleting an account or record removes its active content, but a small marker remains to prevent an offline device from bringing deleted content back when it syncs. Turn off optional measurements to stop future event records. Use Settings to export or delete your records. Offline deletion requests wait until they can sync; exported files are separate copies you control.

Cloud sync capacity is limited in this preview, and online service may be unavailable at times. When that happens, the app indicates the service is unavailable; your local garden remains on your device.

Azure backup settings checked on 10 October 2026 specify backups every four hours, an eight-hour retention setting and storage in more than one geographic region. These settings do not guarantee that a backup will be available or that a complete restore will work. This preview does not promise a recovery service or a recovery deadline. Deleting information from the active database does not immediately remove it from existing backups, and we do not know exactly when providers delete backup copies. A test using a local copy of the service checked that deletion markers remain after an app restart; restoring from Azure backups and keeping those markers after a restore have not been tested. We do not know whether restoring a backup could bring back deleted information. Retention periods for provider security logs, sign-in records and support email copies are also unconfirmed.
