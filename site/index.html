A small rhythm you can repeat

Start with your day, not a deadline.

1. Choose an anchor

Pick something that already happens, like setting down your morning mug. Let that familiar moment be your reminder.

2. Make the action tiny

Try one shoulder roll, one sentence, or putting one thing away. Choose a step small enough for an ordinary, busy day.

3. Celebrate your moment

A quiet smile or a little “there we go” can mark the win. Choose what feels natural to you, not what looks impressive.

What to expect when installing

The current download is not digitally signed. Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” because it does not recognise the publisher. If you downloaded from the Bloomstep links above and are comfortable continuing, choose More info → Run anyway. Otherwise, cancel. Never override your workplace's security rules.

The installer is for your Windows account and does not need administrator rights. You can check the download's checksum on the releases page before installing. A Microsoft Store version is not available yet.

Optional receipt for understanding setup (off by default)

If you choose, keep a receipt in this page's memory and export it to a file. Nothing is saved in browser storage. Closing or reloading the page removes the in-memory receipt. It contains only random event IDs, fixed page/download event names and UTC dates, not URLs or your habits.

A few things you might be wondering.

What if I miss a day?

Your plant does not wilt and your progress is not taken away. Come back when you can, or make the action smaller.

Is Bloomstep free?

The current Windows download is free. There is no subscription or payment step in this download.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

You need to sign in to get started. Once your garden is available on the device, you can work locally during a connection interruption and sync when connected again. Sign-in options depend on the available providers.

Can I take my habits with me or delete them?

Use Settings to export your records as JSON. The app also has deletion controls for habits, private feedback and your cloud account data. Offline deletion requests wait for sync; exported files and provider backups are separate copies.

Is this medical advice?

No. Bloomstep is a habit-building tool, not medical care or treatment. Choose actions that are safe for you. It is designed for ages 16 and up.

Your habits are yours.

No ads, no selling habit data, and no third-party product analytics scripts. Your garden is stored on your device and syncs to your private account when connected. Your habit and feedback text are not included in website counts. The local database relies on your Windows account protections; it is not itself encrypted.

This page sends small, first-party observations: a page view, the main download-button click, and an architecture download click. Only daily counts are saved, with a category such as search, referral, direct, campaign or unknown. Referrer hostnames are classified in the browser, never sent. Only bounded campaign tags are inspected; their values are not stored.

No cookies, browser-storage identifiers, fingerprints, IP addresses or full URLs are collected by this measurement feature. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control turn off sending. Missing and opted-out visits remain unknown, not zero. Counts do not identify unique people or prove successful downloads. Website counts are separate from optional, default-off in-app measurements.

Daily website counts are kept for at most 90 days, with strict request and volume limits. Azure and GitHub provide hosting and downloads and may retain their own standard access/security logs. App event retention is 13 months when you opt in; export and deletion are available in Settings. No automatic browser-to-account matching is performed.

Downloads work independently of optional measurement and service availability.
