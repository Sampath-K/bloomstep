A small rhythm you can repeat

Start with your day, not a deadline.

For people who want a gentle routine alongside their Windows day. For example: after opening your laptop, write one sentence toward a personal project, then mark the moment in your garden. Start with one habit; change the recipe when it stops fitting your day.

1. Choose an anchor

Pick something that already happens, like setting down your morning mug. Let that familiar moment be your reminder.

2. Make the action tiny

After setting down your mug, try one gentle shoulder roll. In Bloomstep, pair the routine and action in your recipe. Make it smaller if it feels difficult; choose an action that is safe for you.

3. Celebrate your moment

A quiet smile or a little “there we go” can mark the win. Choose what feels natural to you. Check in with “did it” in Bloomstep; “not today” keeps your plant's growth.

A few things you might be wondering.

What if I miss a day?

Your plant does not wilt and your progress is not taken away. Come back when you can, or make the action smaller.

Is Bloomstep free?

The current Windows download is free. There is no subscription or payment step in this download.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

You need to sign in to get started. Once your garden is available on the device, you can work locally during a connection interruption and sync when connected again. Sign-in options depend on the available providers.

How can I share feedback or ask for help?

After sign-in, use the app's private feedback option to describe what you wanted to try and where you got stuck. Do not include passwords or sensitive habit details. Feedback is not an email signup, and a reply or response time is not guaranteed. This website does not collect contact details or offer a mailing list.

Can I take my habits with me or delete them?

Use Settings to export your records as JSON. The app also has deletion controls for habits, private feedback and your cloud account data. Offline deletion requests wait for sync; exported files and provider backups are separate copies.

Is this medical advice?

No. Bloomstep is a habit-building tool, not medical care or treatment. Choose actions that are safe for you. It is designed for ages 16 and up.
